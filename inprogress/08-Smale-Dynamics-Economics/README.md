# Smale Problem #08: Dynamical Systems in Economic Theory

## Problem Formulation
Extend the mathematical foundations of general competitive equilibrium (Arrow-Debreu) to incorporate dynamical price adjustment mechanisms, incomplete markets, and non-equilibrium trajectories.

## Mathematical Context
Smale (1976) introduced global analysis methods into mathematical economics, studying the structure of Pareto optima and global Newton methods for price adjustments.

## Research Directions & Open Leads
Tâtonnement dynamics, Global Newton price dynamics, Non-equilibrium thermodynamics analogies.
