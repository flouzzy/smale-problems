# Smale Problem #18: What Are the Limits of Intelligence and Learning?

## Problem Formulation
Establish the foundational mathematical principles of learning, intelligence, and computational optimization in high-dimensional adaptive systems.

## Mathematical Context
Felipe Cucker and Steve Smale (2002, Bulletin of the AMS) founded the mathematical theory of learning using reproducing kernel Hilbert spaces (RKHS) and regularization theory.

## Research Directions & Open Leads
Statistical learning theory (Vapnik-Chervonenkis), Non-convex landscape geometry in deep learning, Generalization bounds in overparameterized regimes.
