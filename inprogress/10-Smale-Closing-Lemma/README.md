# Smale Problem #10: The Closing Lemma for Discrete Dynamical Systems

## Problem Formulation
For a diffeomorphism f of a compact manifold M, can any non-wandering point be made periodic by a small C^r perturbation of f (for r ≥ 2)?

## Mathematical Context
Charles Pugh (1967) proved the C^1 Closing Lemma. The C^r case for r ≥ 2 remains one of the greatest open problems in smooth ergodic theory and dynamical systems.

## Research Directions & Open Leads
Hamiltonian Floer homology, Connecting lemmas (Arnaud, Hayashi), Homoclinic tangencies.
