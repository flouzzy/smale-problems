# Smale Problem #15: Navier-Stokes Existence and Smoothness (Millennium Problem #6)

## Problem Formulation
Do smooth, physically reasonable solutions to the 3D incompressible Navier-Stokes equations exist globally in time for all smooth initial conditions?

## Mathematical Context
Jean Leray (1934) proved the global existence of weak solutions. Millennium Prize Problem #6 asks whether smooth solutions can develop finite-time singularities (blow-up).

## Research Directions & Open Leads
Energy inequalities, Critical Sobolev spaces, Tao's discretized blow-up models, Helicity and vortex dynamics.
