# Smale Problem #02: The Poincaré Conjecture (Resolved)

## Problem Statement
Every simply connected, closed 3-manifold is homeomorphic to the 3-sphere $\mathbb{S}^3$.

## Resolution History
- **Formulated by**: Henri Poincaré (1904), included as Problem 2 in Steve Smale's 2000 list and as Millennium Prize Problem #2 (Clay Mathematics Institute).
- **Proved by**: **Grigori Perelman** in 2002–2003 across three preprints on arXiv:
  1. *The entropy formula for the Ricci flow and its geometric applications* (arXiv:math/0211159).
  2. *Ricci flow with surgery on three-manifolds* (arXiv:math/0303109).
  3. *Finite extinction time for the solutions to the Ricci flow on certain three-manifolds* (arXiv:math/0307245).
- **Technique**: Richard Hamilton's Ricci Flow ($\partial_t g = -2 \operatorname{Ric}$) with geometric surgery and monotonicity of $\mathcal{W}$-entropy.
- **Verification**: Fully verified in 2006 by Morgan-Tian, Cao-Zhu, and Kleiner-Lott. Perelman was awarded the Fields Medal in 2006 and the Clay Millennium Prize in 2010 (both declined).
